ABOUT THE SARKIVE

The Sarkive is an unofficial playable index of public Mr Sark material.

It is built to find, browse and play material spread across stream archives, YouTube uploads and other surviving sources.

THE ARCHIVE

GAME ARCHIVE - Game footage is mapped into content-based chapters. A chapter can use one or more source VOD segments, so the archive follows the game rather than VOD boundaries.

SHOWS + PODCASTS - Respawn Inbox, Radio Respawn, Nerd Poker, X-Play and podcast appearances are indexed as playable collections and episodes.

HOME - Browse the archive through shelves built from the site data.

SEARCH - Searches titles, chapters, episodes, people, tags, descriptions and dates across the archive.

TOOLS

SARK TV - Plays random Twitch clips non-stop.

SARK RADIO - White Bat tracks YouTube identified on archived Mr Sark streams.

MARBLES SCORES - Scores, race results and player standings for archived Marbles on Stream races.

VOD INDEX - Browse preserved full stream VODs by date or search. Duplicate uploads of the same stream are grouped as alternate copies.

CATAGORIES - Browse games by archive tags.

SHOP - Links to Sark's official Spring merch. The Sarkive does not process sales or receive money from purchases.

SARK LINKS - Direct links to Sark's main public accounts, channels and communities.

PALS - Twitch and social links for friends and collaborators found around the archive.

HOW IT WORKS

The Sarkive stores the index, not the video files. Its data records source links and IDs, titles, dates, tags, people, chapters, timestamps, scores and other archive metadata. Playback uses the original YouTube, Archive.org or Twitch source where available.

The index is assembled from surviving public uploads and VODs, stream metadata, transcripts and subtitles, with manual review used to identify content and map useful timestamps.

UNOFFICIAL

The Sarkive is not operated by or affiliated with Mr Sark. It covers public work and public-facing links.
